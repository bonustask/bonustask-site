Find the right work from home job

Remote jobs in Canada

Choose Canada in the country filter to find listed options that accept Canadian residents. Start with Skybridge Americas for customer support or Working Solutions for contractor programs. Check the province and hours before applying.

Entry-level remote jobs

Customer support and online task work are useful places to start. Browse the customer service companies and AI and data platforms. Look for openings that match your skills, even if you have never worked remotely.

Work from home without phone calls

Explore writing, proofreading and transcription if you prefer text or audio tasks to customer calls. Some AI projects also involve reviewing written responses. Check each role for meetings or voice tasks.

Part-time remote work

Contractor support, tutoring and project work can offer part-time hours. Compare virtual assistant roles and online teaching options. Check whether you choose your hours or work fixed shifts.

Worldwide remote work

ProofreadingServices.com welcomes applications from any country. Other companies set locations by role. Use each direct application link to see current openings and working-hour requirements.

Online jobs and extra income

Use the 100-company list for employment and contract work. The featured options include surveys, paid research and AI projects. Surveys and rewards are for extra income, with no guaranteed hours or salary.

How to apply for remote jobs

  1. Choose a company and open its direct application link.
  2. Check the location, pay, schedule and equipment requirements.
  3. Tailor your resume or profile to the role and complete its screening steps.

About this list

BonusTask brings together 100 employers and work platforms with direct application links, plus nine featured earning options repeated in their matching categories. Openings and eligibility can change. Referral links are marked in the featured section.
