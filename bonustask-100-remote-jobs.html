MAKE YOUR SEARCH COUNT

Before you apply

1. Check your location

Remote can mean within one country, province, state or time zone. Read residency and work-authorization requirements before spending time on an application.

2. Match the work

Use company careers pages for employee roles and contractor applications for project work. Treat surveys and paid studies as occasional extra cash.

3. Read the terms

Check pay, fees, equipment, hours and payment methods. Tailor your resume or profile to the role, and keep track of where you apply.

PLAN YOUR APPLICATIONS

Find remote work that matches your location and experience

Remote jobs in Canada: check where the employer can hire

To work remotely from Canada, choose a vacancy that explicitly accepts Canadian residents. A job described as “remote US” usually does not mean you can perform it from a Canadian address. Check the country, province, work-authorization and time-zone requirements in the actual posting.

Start by checking Shopify, S&P Data Digital and Skybridge Americas, then review eligible openings at Concentrix and TTEC. Their available locations depend on the position. For project work, look at the country-specific opportunities from TELUS Digital AI Community and OneForma.

Before submitting, confirm whether compensation is in CAD or another currency, whether the arrangement is employment or contracting, and whether training requires attendance at a physical location.

Entry-level remote jobs and work with no previous remote experience

No previous remote experience is different from no experience at all. Customer service, retail, office administration and volunteer work can provide relevant skills for an entry-level application. Look for the qualifications in the posting rather than assuming a company’s entire hiring list is beginner-friendly.

Check customer-support openings at Foundever, Alorica and Concentrix. For online task work, review Clickworker and CrowdGen by Appen. Eligibility, screening and available assignments still apply; none of these names is a promise of an entry-level opening today.

Show the practical skills you already have: handling customer questions, writing clear emails, learning software and completing work accurately. Include examples or results instead of calling yourself a “remote-work expert.”

Non-phone remote work: writing, transcription and AI tasks

For work without a customer-service phone queue, focus on the actual tasks in the job description. Writing, proofreading, transcription and some AI evaluation projects can be more relevant than a general customer-service vacancy.

Explore Rev and TranscribeMe for transcription, Scribendi for editing, and DataAnnotation or Outlier for AI-related tasks. Some positions still involve meetings, recorded audio, interviews or video onboarding. “Non-phone” does not automatically mean no speaking or no scheduled calls.

Compare the unit of pay carefully. A rate for one hour of recorded audio is not the same as a rate for one hour of your time. Include research, revisions and qualification work when deciding whether a project is worthwhile.

Part-time remote jobs and flexible contractor work

Part-time describes the number of hours; flexible describes who controls the schedule. A part-time support job can require fixed evening or weekend shifts. A project platform may let you choose tasks but offer fewer assignments than you need.

Review Working Solutions, NexRep and Omni Interactions for contractor support programs. For skill-based work, compare the application requirements at Time Etc, Preply and Cambly. Check minimum availability, live-session requirements and whether preparation time is paid.

Choose the arrangement around the hours you can actually commit. If you need predictable monthly income, assess guaranteed hours and contract terms separately from advertised earning potential.

International remote jobs and work-from-anywhere restrictions

A distributed team does not mean every vacancy accepts applicants from every country. Companies may hire in a limited set of countries, require overlap with a particular time zone or restrict where employees can travel while working.

Explore the careers pages for Automattic, GitLab, Remote and Deel. For multilingual projects, check RWS TrainAI, Welocalize and OneForma. Read the location attached to the individual role or project.

Before applying across borders, confirm the permitted work location, payment method and required working hours. Do not use a VPN or a different address to get around a platform’s country rules.

How to apply for remote jobs directly with a company

  1. Choose the right application route. Use the company’s official careers or contributor page linked in this guide. An employer’s own hiring portal is different from a third-party job aggregator.
  2. Read the location and work arrangement first. Confirm fully remote versus hybrid, employee versus contractor, and any in-person training or travel.
  3. Tailor your application. Use the actual role title and describe relevant experience, software and measurable results. Include samples if requested.
  4. Complete assessments yourself. Follow the employer’s rules and make sure your application accurately represents your ability.
  5. Track what you submitted. Save the role, application date and contact details. Recheck the company page if a vacancy disappears.

For customer-support roles, also check headset, internet and quiet-workspace requirements. For project platforms, review qualification steps, payment terms and any disclosed costs before accepting work.

Remote employment, paid surveys and research are different

Survey apps and paid research can provide extra income, but they are not a substitute for a salaried job. AttaPoll, Freecash, Swagbucks and Prime Opinion are featured earning options. User Interviews and Prolific involve qualifying for research. Babel Audio and Mercor offer project-related opportunities with their own screening requirements.

Choose the featured earning options for occasional tasks, or use the 100-company remote-work directory to explore employment and contract applications. For more about survey-based earning, visit the BonusTask survey and rewards guide.
